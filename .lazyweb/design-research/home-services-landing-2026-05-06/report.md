# Home Services Landing Research

## TL;DR
High-performing home service pages are direct: urgent CTA, plain service categories, trust proof, and a short request form. Premium product pages are useful inspiration for AR Air because the HVAC renders can feel like real equipment instead of decoration.

## Current State
![Current State](references/current-state.png)
The current design already has usable assets and colors. The redesign needs calmer composition.

## Patterns To Borrow
- Appointment-led service pages keep booking/call controls persistent and visible.
- Product-led pages let one large object carry the first viewport, then support it with concise copy.
- Service directories use scan-friendly rows/cards, not a dense hero full of competing objects.
- Trust proof works best near the CTA: 24/7 emergency, same-day follow-up, residential/commercial, certified HVAC/electrical.
