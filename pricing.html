How pricing works

Clear numbers first. Final quote after diagnosis.

Are these final prices?

They are common repair prices. Final pricing can change after diagnosis if the equipment needs additional parts, access is limited, or the failure is different than expected.

Why does compressor replacement require booking?

Compressor pricing depends on equipment size, refrigerant type, warranty status, and system condition. AR Air needs to inspect it before giving the right number.
